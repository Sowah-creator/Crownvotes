## 1. Event responsibility
The Organiser is responsible for the event description, categories, contestant eligibility, contestant permissions, judging criteria, prize details, winner selection/confirmation, and fulfilment of prizes. The Organiser warrants that it has authority to run the event and that its content and prizes are lawful.

## 2. Approval conditions
No paid voting may be activated until CrownVotes has reviewed the event and recorded all required written payment-provider, network and regulatory approvals. The Organiser must not advertise paid voting, a shortcode or a prize until activation is confirmed in writing by CrownVotes.

- Organiser share: **[__]%** of gross successful paid-vote collections
- Vote price: **GHS [__] per vote**
- Any payment-provider fees, taxes, refunds, chargebacks, reversals or other deductions: **[state who bears each item and how it is calculated]**

Unless expressly stated otherwise, the platform commission and organiser share are calculated per verified successful transaction. Pending, failed, reversed, refunded or charged-back transactions are not treated as earned revenue. The split shown in the organiser dashboard is a ledger allocation and is not proof that money has been transferred.

## 4. Reconciliation and payout
CrownVotes will reconcile verified transactions and show the Organiser's payable balance. Payout requests are subject to identity and payout-account checks, reconciliation, any agreed reserve/hold period, provider settlement and applicable law. The payout timetable is **[e.g. within __ business days after event close and reconciliation]**. A payout is complete only when the transfer has been made and a transfer reference is recorded.
