CROWNVOTES POLICY TEMPLATE

Terms of Use

Draft template — must be reviewed and approved by a Ghana-qualified lawyer before public launch.

1. Platform role

CrownVotes provides online tools for organisers to publish award events, manage categories and contestants, and receive votes. Each organiser remains responsible for the accuracy, fairness and lawful operation of their event, including prizes, eligibility, judging rules and winner announcements.

2. Organiser approval

Organisers must provide accurate identity, contact and payout information. CrownVotes may request supporting documents, suspend an event, or reject an application where information is incomplete or an event presents legal, payment, safety or fraud concerns.

3. Voting and payments

A vote is counted only after the payment provider confirms a successful transaction and CrownVotes verifies the transaction server-side. Screenshots, payment prompts and browser redirects are not proof of payment. Vote prices, vote limits, event closing times and refund rules must be displayed before checkout.

4. Platform commission and organiser share

The applicable CrownVotes commission is agreed with the organiser and shown in the organiser agreement before paid voting is enabled. Unless the signed event agreement states otherwise, the commission is calculated as a percentage of gross successful vote payments; the remaining balance is credited to the organiser ledger. Gateway fees, taxes, refunds, chargebacks and other deductions must be separately disclosed in the event agreement.

5. Payouts

Organiser balances are payable only after successful payment verification, reconciliation, any stated hold period and completion of required identity and payout checks. A payout request is not a completed transfer. CrownVotes will provide a payout reference and status; a payout is marked paid only after the transfer is confirmed.

6. Prohibited conduct

Users must not automate, manipulate, reverse, duplicate or fraudulently obtain votes; exploit platform defects; impersonate contestants or organisers; or interfere with event results. CrownVotes may suspend suspicious transactions and preserve relevant records for investigation.

7. Refunds and disputes

Event-specific refund and dispute rules must be shown before payment. CrownVotes may reverse votes associated with refunded, reversed, fraudulent or charged-back transactions, subject to applicable law and provider rules.

8. Event and prize compliance

Paid voting involving cash, material prizes, entry fees, chance-based selection or other regulated activity must not be enabled until the organiser and CrownVotes have obtained all required provider, network and regulatory approvals. An organiser must not describe a prize or voting mechanic inaccurately to bypass restrictions.

9. Contact
