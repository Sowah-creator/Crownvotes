CROWNVOTES POLICY TEMPLATE

Privacy Notice

Draft template — complete a Ghana Data Protection Act review and register/obtain advice as required before public launch.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use CrownVotes, we may collect account name, email address, organiser contact details, contestant profile information, vote records, transaction references, payment status, device and security logs, and payout destination details supplied by organisers.

Why we use it

We use information to create accounts, operate award events, process and verify payments, record votes, prevent fraud, reconcile organiser balances, handle support requests and comply with legal and provider requirements.

Payment information

Payment credentials and PINs must never be collected by CrownVotes. Payment processing is handled by the selected payment provider. CrownVotes stores transaction references and limited payment metadata needed to verify the transaction and account for the event.

Sharing

We share information only as needed with event organisers, payment providers, SMS/USSD providers, hosting and security vendors, and competent authorities where legally required. The final notice must identify actual providers and explain what data each receives.

Retention and security

We retain information only for defined operational, dispute, tax, accounting and legal periods. We use access controls, password hashing, encrypted payout destinations, secure hosting and backups. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your rights and contact

Before launch, specify the data controller, contact details, data protection officer (if applicable), lawful bases, retention schedule, request process, complaint route and any cross-border transfers.
